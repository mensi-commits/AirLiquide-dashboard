flow : logistic team bring raw material
| Gaz                                       | Type              | Code produit | Durée de vie                 |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------- |

and either confirm it or reject it after analysing it (it should be conforme to be confirmed)
